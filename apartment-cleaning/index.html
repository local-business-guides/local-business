Start with the rooms you want addressed

Apartment Cleaning is a chance to set out which parts of your home need attention and what you want done in each. Apartments differ in layout and in how residents use their space, so explain your own setup instead of relying on a standard assumption. Ask the business to confirm the tasks it offers and the rooms it can cover during your appointment.

You may want to discuss the kitchen, bathroom, sleeping area, living space, or floors. If only certain rooms are a priority, say so when you first get in touch. A clear room list gives you a practical starting point for discussing the service and what to prepare.

Why a room-by-room plan matters

In an apartment, your priorities may depend on how the space is arranged and which areas see the most daily use. Agreeing on a plan beforehand helps avoid spending time on low-priority tasks while an area you care about is left out. It also gives you a chance to ask about anything that may fall outside the business's usual work.

Before booking, consider these questions:

  • Which rooms and tasks are part of the service?
  • Can you request focus on selected rooms?
  • Are there limits on cleaning inside appliances or cabinets?
  • What access or arrival instructions should you provide?

A common mistake is to expect every apartment to get the same checklist. Another is to leave requests about a particular surface or task until the cleaner arrives. Share those details early, and ask for a clear explanation of what has been agreed.

Make access and preparation straightforward

Write down your priorities in order, especially if you want attention across several rooms. Put away small personal belongings and clear the surfaces you want cleaned. If your building has a call box, a particular entrance, or instructions for visitors, explain how to get in when arranging the visit. Let the business know about any household preferences that could affect the work.

There's no need to rearrange your apartment for the cleaner. Still, moving everyday items out of the way can help make the requested areas accessible. Don't move heavy furniture or handle a difficult cleaning task just to prepare. If you have a concern about a surface, ask what approach is appropriate before the appointment.

Call a professional when a task is taking more time or effort than you can manage, when you aren't sure how to care for a surface, or when you'd rather have help with a set of household tasks. Explain what you need and ask whether it fits the service. That conversation can help you decide whether the proposed plan suits your apartment.
